# Newsletter Template & Examples

This document contains the newsletter template structure and a real example from Peter.

## Template Structure

```
Howdy y'all 👋

[Month YYYY] is [nearly a wrap / here / wrapping up]. Here's what landed in [Month], including [teaser for 1-2 highlight features].

[Major Feature 1 Headline]
[1-2 paragraph description of what it is and what it does. Make it conversational and clear. Explain the context and value. Include specific details about functionality.]

Who's this for?
[Target audience and specific use cases. Be concrete about who benefits and how they'd use it. Provide real-world scenarios.]

[Additional context: early access info, how to enable it, call to action, relevant links, special notes]

[Major Feature 2 Headline]
[Same pattern as Feature 1: clear description, conversational tone, specific details]

Who's this for?
[Target audience and use cases]

[Additional context]

[Continue for all major features - typically 1-3 per month]

A few smaller things
- [Minor update 1 - one line description with context]
- [Minor update 2 - one line description with context]
- [Minor update 3 - one line description with context]
- [Continue for all smaller updates - typically 3-8 items]

What's next
[1-2 paragraphs about upcoming work, areas of focus, things in the pipeline. Be specific where possible but okay to be somewhat general. This section is optional — skip if nothing meaningful to share.]

[FOUNDER'S NOTE - OPTIONAL]
[If provided, include personal note from Peter here. Could be about team growth, company milestones, reflections on product direction, customer appreciation, etc. Keep it personal and genuine.]

On behalf of the whole Accoil team: [closing message that thanks readers, encourages feedback, expresses excitement about the product and working with customers]

Peter
```

## Section Breakdown

### Opening (Required)
- Start with "Howdy y'all 👋"
- Set the context for the month
- Tease the highlights to hook readers
- Keep it brief (2-3 sentences)

### Major Features (1-3 per newsletter)
Each major feature should get its own section with:
- **Clear headline** - Either feature name or benefit-focused
- **Description** - What it is, what it does (1-2 paragraphs)
- **"Who's this for?" section** - Specific audience and use cases
- **Additional context** (optional) - Links, CTAs, early access info, special notes

What makes something a "major feature":
- Significant new functionality
- High-value capability for users
- Something worth highlighting and explaining in detail
- Deserves its own section vs. one-line mention

### A Few Smaller Things (Required if there are minor updates)
- Bulleted list format
- One line per update
- Brief but clear — say what it is and why it matters
- Typically 3-8 items
- Can include: bug fixes, small improvements, UI updates, minor features

### What's Next (Optional)
- Preview upcoming work and focus areas
- Can be specific (named features coming soon) or general (areas of investment)
- Sets expectations for what's coming
- 1-2 paragraphs
- Skip this section if there's nothing meaningful to share

### Founder's Note (Optional)
- Personal message from Peter
- Could be about team, company milestones, product philosophy, gratitude
- Keep it genuine and relevant
- Only include if there's something worth saying

### Closing (Required)
- "On behalf of the whole Accoil team:" followed by warm closing
- Thank readers, encourage feedback, express excitement
- Sign as "Peter"
- 1-2 sentences

## Example Newsletter

Below is a real example newsletter to reference for voice, structure, and style:

---

**Subject: Accoil Product Update — [Month Year]**

Howdy y'all 👋

January is nearly a wrap, and we shipped some big updates. Here's what landed this month, including AI-powered account summaries and a major upgrade to activation tracking.

**AI Account Summaries**

We built AI summaries for every account in Accoil. Instead of manually reviewing dashboards to understand what's happening, you get a plain-language summary that explains what changed, why it matters, and what you should do about it.

The AI pulls together engagement trends, recent activity, signal alerts, and context to give you the full picture in seconds. It's like having an analyst review every account for you.

Who's this for?
Customer Success teams preparing for check-ins, renewal conversations, or QBRs. Instead of spending 10+ minutes per account reviewing data, you get the context you need in 30 seconds.

This feature is rolling out to all customers this week. You'll see AI summaries on account detail pages and in weekly digest emails.

**Activation Tracking 2.0**

We rebuilt activation tracking from the ground up. The new version supports multi-step activation milestones, custom activation criteria per segment, and clear visibility into where trial users and new accounts get stuck.

You can now define activation as a series of steps (not just a single event), track progress across each milestone, and see exactly where drop-off happens. This makes it way easier to identify friction points and improve your onboarding flow.

Who's this for?
Product teams and Customer Success teams focused on getting trial users and new customers to their first value moment. Especially useful if your activation process has multiple steps (like setup → first integration → first insight).

Check out the new Activation dashboard to set up your milestones. We also published a guide on activation best practices [link].

**Better Slack Notifications**

We redesigned how Accoil sends notifications to Slack. You now get richer context in each alert (including AI-generated summaries when relevant), better formatting for readability, and the ability to take action directly from Slack (like snoozing an alert or adding a note).

Who's this for?
Anyone using Slack notifications for engagement alerts. The new format makes it faster to understand what's happening and decide what to do about it.

A few smaller things
- Improved performance on company list pages (50% faster load times for large lists)
- Added ability to export segment results to CSV
- New filter options for the Signals feed (filter by signal type, severity, or date range)
- Fixed bug where some Intercom integration syncs were failing
- Added support for custom date ranges in engagement score charts
- Improved mobile experience for account detail pages

What's next

We're focused on three big areas in February:

First, we're adding predictive churn scoring — using AI to identify accounts likely to churn in the next 90 days based on engagement patterns. This will give Customer Success teams even earlier warning signals.

Second, we're expanding integrations. HubSpot workflows integration is nearly ready (you'll be able to trigger HubSpot workflows based on Accoil signals), and we're adding deeper Salesforce sync capabilities.

Third, we're investing in activation analytics — more reports, cohort analysis, and funnel views to help you understand and improve your activation rates.

On behalf of the whole Accoil team: thanks for being part of this journey. We're building Accoil for you, so if you have feedback or ideas, we'd love to hear from you. Rock'n'roll.

Peter

---

## Template Notes

**Structure flexibility:**
- Number of major features varies (1-3 typical)
- "A few smaller things" can be skipped if there aren't any
- "What's next" is optional but valuable for setting expectations
- Founder's note is optional — only use when there's something meaningful to say

**Length:**
- Major features: 2-4 paragraphs each (including "Who's this for?")
- Minor updates: One line each
- Total newsletter: Typically 600-1000 words
- Don't worry about exact length — focus on clarity and completeness

**Tone reminders:**
- Conversational, not corporate
- First person from Peter
- Clear and helpful
- Specific and concrete
- Warm and appreciative

**Common patterns:**
- "We built..." / "We shipped..." to introduce features
- "Who's this for?" always as its own section
- Action items and links embedded naturally
- Closing thanks readers and invites feedback
