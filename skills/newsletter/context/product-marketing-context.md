*Last updated: 2026-02-01*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** The engagement and insights layer built for B2B SaaS

**What it does:** Accoil turns raw product usage data into clear engagement signals, health scores, and actionable insights. It helps B2B SaaS teams see which accounts are thriving, which are slipping, and what changed — so they can spot risk early, guide users to value, and find expansion opportunities without digging through dashboards.

**Product category:** Customer engagement analytics / Product-led growth analytics / Customer health monitoring

**Product type:** B2B SaaS platform

**Business model:** Subscription-based pricing on Monthly Active Accounts (MAA). Growth plan starts at $399/month (up to 500 MAA), Scale plan at $899/month (up to 1,500 MAA). Additional accounts billed at tiered rates. 30-day free trial available. We also offer consulting services from about $10,000 to $25,000-$30,000 U.S. to implement the signals and help teams address things like net revenue retention, gross revenue retention, etc.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** B2B teams can't easily tell which accounts are healthy vs. at-risk. Product usage data sits in analytics tools that aren't built for account-level health monitoring. Teams resort to spreadsheets, manual exports, and reactive firefighting.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **Product analytics tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog):** Built for event analysis and funnel optimization, not account health monitoring. Require analysts to create reports. Don't integrate with CRM/CS workflows.
- **Customer success platforms (Gainsight, ChurnZero, Vitally):** Expensive, complex to implement, over-engineered for most teams. Often don't handle activation well.
- **CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot):** Not designed for product usage data. Manual data entry. Lag behind real-time behavior.
- **Spreadsheets and manual processes:** Don't scale. Stale data. Requires constant manual work.

**What it costs them:**
- Lost revenue from churn that could have been prevented with early intervention
- Missed expansion opportunities because they can't identify engaged accounts
- Wasted time building and maintaining custom dashboards and reports
- Poor cross-team alignment when product, CS, and sales work from different data sources

**Emotional tension:**
- Anxiety about renewals ("Are we going to be surprised by churn again?")
- Frustration with data silos and manual work ("Why is this so hard?")
- Fear of missing early warning signs ("What are we not seeing?")
- Stress from reactive firefighting vs. proactive account management

**Indirect competitors (conflicting approach):**

## Objections & Anti-Personas
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have [analytics tool]. Why do we need this?" | Analytics tools are built for product teams analyzing funnels and features. Accoil is built for go-to-market teams monitoring account health. It works alongside your analytics, not instead of it — and surfaces insights where your CS/sales teams actually work (Slack, CRM). |
| "We don't have perfect event tracking yet." | You don't need perfect tracking to start. Accoil helps you identify the key events that matter for engagement and activation. You can start with basic events and refine over time as you learn what drives value. |
| "This seems expensive for what it does." | Compare the cost to (a) a single prevented churn, (b) one expansion deal identified early, or (c) the analyst time saved. Most customers see ROI in the first month. Plus, our MAA pricing is predictable — unlike event-based or MAU-based pricing that can spike unexpectedly. |

**Anti-persona:**
- **Early-stage pre-product-market-fit startups** - Not enough customers yet to justify engagement monitoring; should focus on product development and customer discovery first
- **B2C or consumer-focused companies** - Account-based model doesn't apply; would need user-centric tool instead
- **Companies with <50 customers** - Manual processes still work at this scale; Accoil makes sense once manual tracking breaks down
- **Teams that need white-glove enterprise features** - If you require on-premise deployment, custom SLAs, or extensive professional services, enterprise CS platforms like Gainsight may be better fit despite higher cost
- **Companies without any event tracking** - Need basic product analytics instrumentation first (can be simple); Accoil works with existing tracking, not as replacement for it

## Switching Dynamics

**Push (what drives them away from current solution):**
- Spreadsheet chaos and manual data pulls taking hours every week
- Reactive churn surprises instead of early warning signals
- Analytics dashboards that product team uses but CS team can't understand
- Gainsight/enterprise platform too expensive or complex for their stage
- Lack of visibility into activation progress for trials and new users

**Pull (what attracts them to Accoil):**
- Simple engagement scores (0-100) that the whole team can understand
- AI summaries that explain what changed without needing an analyst
- Pricing based on MAA (their business model) not events or users
- Fast setup (2 weeks not 6 months) means quick wins
- Works with their existing data infrastructure (Segment, PostHog, etc.)
- Integrates where their teams work (Slack, HubSpot, Intercom, etc.)

**Habit (what keeps them stuck):**
- "Our current process works well enough" (even though it's manual)
- Already invested time in custom dashboards or reports
- Team trained on existing system (switching costs)
- Fear of migrating data or losing historical context
- "Maybe we can just hire an analyst to solve this"

**Anxiety (what worries them about switching):**
- "What if implementation takes longer than promised?"
- "Will we lose historical data or context?"
- "Will our team actually use another tool?"
- "How hard is it to get our event data into Accoil?"
- "What if we outgrow it and have to switch again?"

**How to address anxiety:**
- 30-day free trial with real data (not toy demo)
- Clear implementation timeline: 2 weeks to value
- Works with existing event data sources (no migration required)
- Revenue Signals Sprint offering for hands-on setup support
- Transparent pricing tiers with clear upgrade path
- Case studies showing adoption across CS, product, and sales teams
