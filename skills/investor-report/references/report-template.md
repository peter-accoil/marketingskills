# Investor Report Template

This template provides the structure for quarterly investor updates. Adapt sections based on what's relevant — not every section needs to appear every quarter.

## Template

```

## TL;DR

- [Highlight 1 — the single most important win or milestone]
- [Highlight 2 — another key win or achievement]
- [Lowlight or challenge — be upfront about what didn't go well]
- [Forward-looking — what's ahead next quarter]

[Add more as needed, but 3-5 is the sweet spot.]
```

## Section Guidelines

### TL;DR
- 3-4 bullets maximum
- Lead with the best news
- Include at least one challenge (shows self-awareness)
- End with a forward-looking statement
- This should be readable in 15 seconds

### Highlights
- 3-5 items
- Each should have a clear "so what" — why does this matter?
- Include numbers where possible
- Order by impact, not chronology

### Lowlights
- 2-3 items minimum (investors distrust updates with zero bad news)
- Frame as: what happened + why + what you learned or are doing about it
- Don't catastrophize, but don't minimize either
- Lowlights with clear lessons or action plans build confidence

### Product Update
- Focus on outcomes and customer impact, not just features shipped
- If you have metrics, show quarter-over-quarter trends
- Technical updates only if they're strategically meaningful

### Sales & Growth
- Lead with revenue/pipeline narrative
- Include customer stories or patterns when possible
- Be honest about what's not working in marketing/sales

### Key Metrics
- Only include metrics you're confident in
- Always include direction (up/down/flat) and context
- If a metric looks bad, explain why and what you're doing about it
- Missing metrics are better than fabricated ones

### Next Quarter
- 3-5 priorities maximum — if everything is a priority, nothing is
- Be clear about pivots or direction changes and why
- Include risks to show strategic thinking

### Investor Asks
- Most under-utilized section — make it count
- Specific > vague (always)
- "Intro to [type of person] at [type of company]" > "help with sales"
- Include enough context that investors can act without follow-up
- 2-4 asks is the sweet spot

### Closing
- Keep it brief and genuine
- Thank investors for their support
- Set an optimistic but realistic tone
